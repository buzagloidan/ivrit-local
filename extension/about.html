Ivrit Local

Hebrew dictation, on your device. Version 0.2.3.

Privacy

Speech recognition runs locally using the model bundled in this extension. No audio or transcripts are sent to a server. No account, analytics, or tracking is included.

Microphone or selected browser-tab audio is held in memory only during recognition. Browser-tab mode opens Chrome’s sharing picker for each session: choose a Chrome tab and enable Share tab audio. Chrome also supplies a video track; this extension disables it and never reads, records, or uploads video frames. Only audio goes to the speech model. Stop, Stop sharing, or closing the panel releases the capture. Imported files are decoded in memory. Transcripts remain in the panel by default. Optional local recovery stores one draft for up to 7 days; it is never synchronized through Chrome Sync. Copy places text on your system clipboard; Insert places text on the webpage you choose. Once inserted, that webpage controls what happens to the text.

Permissions

  • Tab audio sharing: requested through Chrome’s sharing picker only when you choose Browser tab. It does not use the microphone.
  • Microphone access: requested only when you choose to record.
  • Side panel: displays the recorder and transcript.
  • Scripting and optional website access: Insert first uses temporary tab access. You can explicitly allow one website or opt into all HTTP/HTTPS websites when needed. The extension injects its placement guide only when you click Insert. It does not scan your browsing history or run on every page in the background. You can revoke website access in Chrome’s extension settings.
  • Clipboard write: copies the transcript at your request.

Speech model

zipformer2-66m-streaming-he by thewh1teagle. INT8 ONNX export, 66 million parameters. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Model weights are unchanged. Revision: 6c07526ebfdfc71d12ed4ce6aaebe32774a2613a.

Base model: Zengwei/icefall-asr-librispeech-streaming-zipformer-2023-05-17; icefall code is Apache-2.0. The model author credits ivrit.ai for Hebrew training audio. See the bundled model card for details.

Recognition runtime

sherpa-onnx v1.13.7 by the k2-fsa contributors, licensed under Apache-2.0. Its WebAssembly build includes ONNX Runtime, kaldifeat, and other dependencies. Bundled third-party notices are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

The generated loader was modified to remove the example English model preloader. Ivrit Local loads the bundled Hebrew model files instead. The WebAssembly engine binary and recognition API wrapper are unchanged.

Limitations

Recognition may make mistakes; check names and numbers. English recognition is limited. Complex editors and cross-origin frames may require using Copy instead of Insert.
